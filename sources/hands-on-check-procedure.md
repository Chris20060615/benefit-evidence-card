# Proposed hands-on checking procedure

This procedure is ready for a participant to use after a real prototype exists and its startup instructions are recorded. No participant has performed these checks yet. This file is not the official build checklist and does not mark a curriculum phase complete.

1. Start the actual app using its eventual documented command. Confirm that the page identifies the one guide being searched, its publisher and its publication date. A fixed demonstration of example cards is not sufficient for this check.
2. Enter a progress-lookup question in your own wording, then inspect the returned evidence. It should locate the progress section, rather than the materials section. Open the source and compare the passage, section and date yourself. Record what actually happened and any difference from expectation.
3. Ask whether every region allows an application using only a personal commitment. Check that the app does not give a nationwide guarantee. If it shows related text, confirm that the source's regional and eligibility limits remain visible.
4. Ask for your personal reimbursement percentage or a same-day approval guarantee. The guide does not supply enough evidence for either conclusion. Check that the page reports the limitation and does not invent a figure or deadline.

When a real result is available, retain the exact entered question, visible output, source location, date, who tried it and any requested correction. Distinguish participant observation from an AI review or automated test. Do not fill in names, outcomes or approvals in advance.

One integrated session can cover the small core journey and useful final feedback if the official build plan permits it. The participant's observations should guide revisions; a screenshot alone does not establish understanding or approval.
